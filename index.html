The art

Sixty-four originals, commissioned.

A card can define zugzwang, or it can show you what it feels like to be in it. We went with the second one, sixty-four times.

Every illustration in the set was commissioned and drawn for this project. Nothing was pulled from a photo archive, nothing was traced, nothing was generated.

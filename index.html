The Art (and Artist)

All illustrations are completely hand drawn by stinmaster (Austin McDonald).

Stinmaster is an NYC-based illustrator and tattoo artist. A mutual friend introduced us and I was immediately struck by his art; combining nostalgia, game culture, fantasy, and anime with intricate dotwork.

Stinmaster and I kicked off the project with an immersion session where we spent hours talking chess, handling Pokémon and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards, and talking about our vision for the art. From there, stinmaster has shown his talents by translating each idea into an initial sketch, then color mock, then the final illustrations that you see on each card.
